# Full-rounded buttons and field shells

All buttons and button-like controls use `rounded-full` (`Button`, `IconButton`, Pagination, Dialog/Drawer/Toast/Popover close, banner dismiss/action, sidebar menu items, nav triggers). Field shells (`Input`, `Select`, `Combobox`, `NumberField`, `DatePicker`) are also `rounded-full`. Dropdown popovers, `Textarea`, and card-like pickers (`SelectBox`) stay non-pill.
